A FEW GOOD THINGS TO KNOW

Questions, meet answers.

Everything you need to know before bringing the kit home.

What exactly will I receive?

You’ll receive two downloadable PDF editions: US Letter and A4. Each edition contains 28 pages, including a cover, contents page, and printable worksheets.

Is this a physical product?

No. This is a digital product. No physical item will be shipped.

Can I print only the pages I need?

Absolutely. Print the pages you want, whenever you need them. For the intended page dimensions, choose 100% or “Actual Size” in your printer settings.

Can I use the PDF on a tablet?

You can open the PDF in a compatible PDF reader or note-taking app. Available annotation features depend on the app you use.

Can I share the files with my whole book club?

Your purchase is for your personal use. Please don’t redistribute, resell, or share the original files with others.

How will I receive my download?

Checkout and digital delivery are handled securely by Gumroad. Follow the download instructions provided after purchase.
